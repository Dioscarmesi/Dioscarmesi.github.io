Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad

Last updated: 2026-11-21

What we collect

When you connect your Patreon account to Golden VR Salon we receive, through Patreon's official API, only: your Patreon user ID, your display name, and your membership status and tier for our campaign. We may also store the VRChat display name you link to it.

Why

Only to give supporters their perks inside our VRChat world and community (roles, access, name on the supporter board).

What we don't do

We never receive or store your password or payment details. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your membership is active. You can revoke access at any time from your Patreon settings, or ask us on Discord to delete your data.
